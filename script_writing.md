# Writing the script (when the user has only a topic, or wants a script checked)

A reel in this style is a short spoken story about one myth, habit or fact: a problem the viewer lives with, why the
usual answer fails, the real reason (shown with props), the fix, and a calm ending. The pictures are planned from the
words, so the script decides everything that follows.

In the AST_BTS project, `08_Styles/00_SCRIPT_RULES.md` wins on voice (his real phrases). Read it first there.

## Length → words

Natural Hinglish narration runs about **2 words per second** (the chai voice-over: 80 words in 39 s).

| Reel | Words (about) | Lines (6–14 words each) |
|---|---|---|
| 30 s | 60 | 6–8 |
| 45 s | 90 | 9–11 |
| 60 s | 120 | 12–15 |
| 90 s | 180 | 18–22 |

## The 6 steps, in order (label each line with its step)

| Step | What it does | Share of the reel | Chai example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Pain** | The first line is a habit or situation the viewer already lives: "haan, main bhi aisa karta hoon". | first 3–5 s | "Khane ke turant baad ek garam chai… India mein har ghar ki aadat hai." |
| **2. Poke** | Question the habit: what is it secretly doing? Add one real, sourced number. | ~20% | "Par kya aapko pata hai, aapke pet mein ye chai roz kya karti hai? India mein 57% se zyada logon mein iron ki kami hai." |
| **3. Reason** | The mechanism, simply, in objects the viewer can picture (tannin "locks" iron). | ~30% | "Chai mein hota hai tannin. Jaise hi khana aur chai saath milte hain, tannin iron ko pakad ke lock kar deta hai." |
| **4. Fix** ★ | The simple practical fix. **★ Mark this first Fix line: the music switches from dark to calm here.** | ~20% | "To chai chhodni nahi hai, bas khane aur chai ke beech 1 ghante ka gap rakhna hai." |
| **5. Recap / payoff** | Back to the first line's habit, now solved; the good result. | ~10% | "Khana alag, chai alag, tabhi dono ka poora fayda milega." |
| **6. CTA** (optional) | Follow / subscribe with a reason, only if the user gave an ending line. | last 2–3 s | "Aise aur myths ke liye follow kar lena." |

## How the lines should sound
- **Spoken, not written.** One thought per line, 6–14 words, joined with "to / aur / par / bas / ab". No long sentences with two commas.
- **Talk to "aap"** (Hinglish) or "you" (English). Ask a question and answer it: "Iska result? Aapki body 60% tak kam iron absorb kar paati hai."
- **Things, not abstractions.** Every line should give the picture something to *do*: a prop to drop, a number to count, a lock to close, a map to fill. If a line has nothing to show, rewrite it so it does.
- **Repeat the key word** instead of a pronoun ("tannin… tannin iron ko…"). It becomes the title.
- **Numbers only with a real source.** Write the source next to the line in the script ("57% = NFHS-5, women 15–49"). A number from nowhere gets no card, or gets cut.
- **No fear-mongering, no medical advice beyond the simple fix.** Say "doctor se baat karein" for anything serious.

## Checks before showing the script
- [ ] The first line is the Pain (no greeting, no channel name first).
- [ ] Word count fits the length (table above, ±10%).
- [ ] Every line has something to show.
- [ ] Every number has its source written next to it.
- [ ] The first Fix line is marked ★.
- [ ] It sounds right read aloud at a calm pace (read it once yourself at 2 words per second).
