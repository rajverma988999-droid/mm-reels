The reels live **outside** the skill folder, in `../reference_videos/` (`ref_01.mp4` … `ref_12.mp4`, 720×1280,
30 fps, 103–161 s). His old flat paper-style reel is in the project at `09_Videos/Copy MeanWhileMayank/referance/my_older_paper_style/oldPaperStyle.mp4`. They are another

the skill.

windows (its slow winter shots), just over the 5% limit. Aim higher than the gate, not just past it.

## The pattern all 12 share
1. Hook: 3 quick real-world actions in the first 4 s, then a question title (gold).
2. Evidence: a physical map, a clipping card or a counter plate on a desk.
3. Mechanism: macro / medical worlds (emerald, burgundy, indigo) alternating with simple props on mustard.
4. Takeaway: a physical prop with a tick or a cross, then a calm warm last shot.
5. Every shot carries the notebook overlay (crease at 62.5%, 7 holes) and a top-left two-line title.
